      BEFORE YOU NEED IT

      Use these questions to start a conversation with your household. Open each one and discuss what would work for you.

      01 Who would you contact?

      Discuss who each person would contact if your household became separated. Check that everyone knows where to find the contact details you agree to use.

      Does your plan depend on remembering a number or having a charged phone?

      02 What would you take?

      Look through the household checklist together. Discuss which supplies you already have, what needs replacing and which personal needs your list should include.

      Would the same list work for every person in your household?

      Review your checklist
      03 Who might need support?

      Ask household members what support they would want. Consider communication, mobility and individual needs, rather than assuming the same arrangements will work for everyone.

      Who could help if your usual support person was not nearby?
